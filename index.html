Good questions

A few things you might be wondering.

Here's what to know before requesting the Noctra Craving Reset.

What exactly is the Noctra Craving Reset?

It's a Windows desktop tool designed to encourage brief pauses and check-ins throughout your day. Its purpose is to help you pay attention to possible circumstances and habits surrounding sugar cravings. It is an awareness tool, not a diagnostic or treatment tool.

Is it a PDF, website, or Windows application?

It's a Windows desktop tool intended to be installed and used on a compatible Windows computer. The illustrations on this page are design previews, not screenshots of the actual application.

How do I receive it?

Submit the request form with your name and email address. Noctra Wellness will follow up with you about access. Delivery is handled manually, so the form does not provide an instant download.

Why is it free?

We want people to experience the Noctra approach without an upfront purchase. The tool offers a practical starting point for building awareness. You can request it without agreeing to receive optional marketing emails.

Will it make my sugar cravings disappear?

No particular result can be guaranteed. Cravings can be influenced by many factors, and this tool is intended to encourage awareness rather than promise to eliminate them. Its usefulness will depend on the individual and how the tool fits into their routine.

Do I have to subscribe to emails?

No. Optional educational and promotional emails have a separate, unchecked consent box. You can request the tool without opting into those emails.

Is Noctra a diet or weight-loss program?

Noctra Wellness focuses on understanding sugar cravings and the behaviors and circumstances that may surround them. The approach emphasizes curiosity, awareness, and practical education rather than restrictive diet rules or shame.

Can I use it on a Mac or phone?

This version is intended for Windows desktop computers. It is not advertised as a native Mac, iPhone, or Android application.
