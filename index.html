The Noctra approach

Your cravings have a context.
Let's pay attention to it.

Cravings don't always happen at convenient times. They can show up between meetings, after a difficult day, during an afternoon slump, or when the house finally gets quiet. The circumstances surrounding them can be worth noticing.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

A few details about Noctra, the desktop tool, and what to expect when you request it.

What exactly is the Noctra Craving Reset?

It's a Windows desktop tool designed to bring reminders about craving awareness into your day. The aim is to help you pause and pay attention to what might be influencing your cravings, rather than relying only on memory or willpower.

Why would I install it on my PC?

Your computer is already part of your daily routine. Having a desktop companion means reminders can bring your attention back to the process while you work or carry on with everyday tasks, instead of expecting you to remember to revisit a guide.

How do I get the tool?

Complete the request form with your name and email address. Noctra will contact you about access and the next steps for getting started.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no payment required to request the free Craving Reset. Noctra may offer additional paid resources in the future, but you do not need to purchase those to request this tool.

Will it stop me from craving sugar?

No tool can guarantee that cravings will disappear. Noctra is designed to support awareness and reflection around the circumstances that may accompany cravings. Your experience will depend on your individual situation.

Is Noctra a diet or medical treatment?

No. Noctra provides educational information about cravings, habits, and everyday behavior. It is not a diet plan or a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
